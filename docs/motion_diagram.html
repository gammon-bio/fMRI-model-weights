Can an agent find and fix a shortcut from the weights alone?

A small transformer learns to predict a planted diagnosis through a scanner carrier instead of brain signal. An agent gets the weights and no data, edits them once, and a sealed test scores the result. Pick an edit and watch where the carrier goes. Every dot is a real episode from the repo.

Where the project stands

  1. PHASE 1Build and gate the testbedMixedNetworks recovered (0.97), site decodable (1.00), shortcut measurable. Does not beat the best linear baseline, reported as a failed gate.
  2. PHASE 2 · MAINRepair from weights, 30 episodesSupportedMean ΔAUC +0.070, p = 0.003. Matched random edits were nearly inert, so this behaves like a sign test.
  3. PHASE 2 · CONFIRMATIONFresh seeds, 27 valid episodesMixedCarrier removal beats other edits (p = 0.004). Overall agent gain did not clearly replicate: +0.029, CI −0.002 to 0.060.
  4. FABLE 5.2 PILOTStronger agent, 10 episodesNo difference4/10 vs 7/27 found the edit, p = 0.44. Exploratory.
  5. BRAINLM · STAGE 0111M-parameter fMRI modelFailedShortcut gate failed. The positive control sat in a subspace that was the same in every seed.
  6. BRAINLM · STAGE 0bFour carriers, balanced samplingMixedShortcut passed (headroom 0.19). Control failed: a random in-band edit repairs almost as much.
  7. BRAINLM · STAGE 0cGround-truth site subspaceMixedRepair lever works in 2 of 3 seeds and flips sign in the third. The mechanism metric saturates.
  8. NEXT · STAGE 1Confound planted in ABIDE scansPlannedNeeds a new mechanism metric, a damage gate, and power for seed-level sign flips before the lock.
